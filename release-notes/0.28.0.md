GeoPhotos 0.28.0 — tracks: shown on the map, and photos placed by them.

**Install:** download `GeoPhotos-0.28.0-win-x64.zip` below, unpack it, run `GeoPhotos.exe`. The program is not signed: if Windows shows *"Windows protected your PC"*, click *More info → Run anyway*. If ExifTool is missing, click **Install for GeoPhotos** at the first start. **Update:** close GeoPhotos and unpack the new zip over the old folder; settings and ExifTool stay. Details — [Installation](https://github.com/rejgan318/geophotos#installation) in the README.

**New since 0.25.0**
- **Tracks** (GPX, KML, KMZ, FIT, TCX, GeoJSON): in the strip by their start time with their shape as the thumbnail; on the map as coloured lines with waypoints as flags. Hovering a line shows the time, altitude and, when the file has them, heart rate, speed, cadence, temperature, power.
- **Track view** (Space on a track): summary — distance, time moving, stops, speeds, ascent and descent, heart rate — and the altitude profile over the dimmed map.
- **Placing by a track:** for the current photo without coordinates a red ring on the track shows where you were when it was taken (by its time and time zone); a photo dropped next to the ring, or a double-click next to it, lands exactly there.
- Photos dragged onto a track in the strip each get their own place on it by their shooting time; those taken outside the track stay as they are.
- While photos are dragged in the strip, thumbnails they cannot be dropped on fade.
- Ctrl+Del removes coordinates, address and altitude of the selected photos; Del now always takes photos or a track off the strip (asking first if there are unsaved changes).
- Folders open several times faster (122 phone photos: 0.6 s instead of 4; 1,713 RAW files: 7 s instead of 39).

Windows 10/11, 64-bit. Interface: English, Russian, Ukrainian.
