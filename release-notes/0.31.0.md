GeoPhotos 0.31.0 — the badges on thumbnails now tell their values.

**Install:** download `GeoPhotos-Setup-0.31.0.exe` below and run it: it installs for you only, without administrator rights. Or take `GeoPhotos-0.31.0-win-x64.zip` and unpack it. The program is not signed: if Windows shows *"Windows protected your PC"*, click *More info → Run anyway*. If ExifTool is missing, click **Install for GeoPhotos** at the first start. **Update:** GeoPhotos 0.30.0 installed with the installer updates itself — the status bar says *"Version 0.31.0 is ready — restart"*. The zip version tells about it and opens this page; unpack the new zip over the old folder, settings and ExifTool stay. Details — [Installation](https://github.com/rejgan318/geophotos#installation) in the README.

**New in 0.31.0**
- The tooltip of a badge on a thumbnail is its value: the pin shows the coordinates, the house the address, the mountain the altitude, the film the length of a video (it used to say just *"Has coordinates"*, *"Has an address"*…).
- The README starts with a few words on why GeoPhotos exists: the track from your watch or phone, the photos from the phone and from the camera — together on one map.

Windows 10/11, 64-bit. Interface: English, Russian, Ukrainian.
