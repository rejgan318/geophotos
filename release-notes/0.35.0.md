GeoPhotos 0.35.0 — the strip on the left, a grid of thumbnails, a context menu, one window.

**Install:** download `GeoPhotos-Setup-0.35.0.exe` below and run it: it installs for you only, without administrator rights. Or take `GeoPhotos-0.35.0-win-x64.zip` and unpack it. The program is not signed: if Windows shows *"Windows protected your PC"*, click *More info → Run anyway*. If ExifTool is missing, click **Install for GeoPhotos** at the first start. **Update:** GeoPhotos 0.30.0 or later installed with the installer updates itself — the status bar says *"Version 0.35.0 is ready — restart"*. The zip version tells about it and opens this page; unpack the new zip over the old folder, settings and ExifTool stay. Details — [Installation](https://github.com/rejgan318/geophotos#installation) in the README.

**New since 0.32.0**
- **The strip on the left:** *View → Strip Position → Bottom / Left*. On a wide monitor the map keeps more height. On the left, ↑ ↓ move along the strip (← → still work too). The choice is remembered.
- **Grid of thumbnails:** G or *View → Grid* shows the strip over the whole window, in rows — handy for picking among many photos. ↑ ↓ go a row up and down, Home / End to the first and last photo, Space opens the photo (Space again — back to the grid), a double click or C shows the photo on the map. G or Esc — back to the map. Ctrl+wheel or Ctrl + «+» / «−» make the grid thumbnails larger or smaller (Ctrl+0 — normal); the size is remembered.
- **PageUp / PageDown** move the selection by a screen of photos — in the grid and in the strip; the strip follows.
- **Context menu:** right-click a thumbnail or a marker on the map (or press the Menu key / Shift+F10): show on the map, copy, paste and delete coordinates, take off the strip, and new — *Show in Explorer* and *Open in Another Program*.
- **Start screen:** when nothing is open — *Open Folder…*, the recent folders and a hint about dropping.
- **One window:** starting GeoPhotos again brings the open window forward; a folder or photos dropped onto its icon or shortcut open there.
- **Recent folders on the taskbar icon:** right-click the GeoPhotos icon in the taskbar or Start — a click opens the folder, even when the program is closed.
- **Shooting direction:** if the phone wrote where the camera looked (iPhone does), the current photo's marker shows a quiet sector that way, when the map is zoomed in to the streets.
- **Dark everywhere:** the menu bar, title bar, scrollbars and Windows dialogs (file choice, messages) are dark too, whatever the Windows theme.
