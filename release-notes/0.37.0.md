GeoPhotos 0.37.0 — Undo, favorite places, Google Maps colors, a resizable strip.

**Install:** download `GeoPhotos-Setup-0.37.0.exe` below and run it: it installs for you only, without administrator rights. Or take `GeoPhotos-0.37.0-win-x64.zip` and unpack it. The program is not signed: if Windows shows *"Windows protected your PC"*, click *More info → Run anyway*. If ExifTool is missing, click **Install for GeoPhotos** at the first start. **Update:** GeoPhotos 0.30.0 or later installed with the installer updates itself — the status bar says *"Version 0.37.0 is ready — restart"*. The zip version tells about it and opens this page; unpack the new zip over the old folder, settings and ExifTool stay. Details — [Installation](https://github.com/rejgan318/geophotos#installation) in the README.

**New since 0.35.0**
- **Undo and Redo** of coordinate changes: Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y (or Ctrl+Shift+Z), also *Edit → Undo / Redo*. One step is one action: a marker that took 12 photos brings all 12 back. Works until you save; the status bar says what was undone.
- **Places — Ctrl+F from anywhere:** the search field with the places you went to lately and your favorite places below a line. The first row is lit — Enter goes there; a digit and Enter go to that place of the history. After a place is chosen the field is empty again and the map takes «+» / «−» at once.
- **Favorite places:** the star next to a place in the list, or *Add to Favorites* in the menu of the mark. They are always on the map as a yellow disc with a star; drag them to move, right-click to copy the coordinates, open in Google Maps, rename or remove. **Ctrl+1…9** — straight to the favorite place with that number. Ctrl+Z undoes changes of the favorites too.
- **The mark:** put by the search or by a right click on the map. Drag it; right-click — copy its coordinates, open it in Google Maps, add it to favorites (named after the found place or the address of the point — the name is ready to be edited). Photos dropped next to the mark or a favorite place land exactly on it — also a marker dragged on the map.
- **Strip size:** drag the edge between the map and the strip, or Ctrl+wheel over the strip, or Ctrl + «+» / «−» (Ctrl+0 — normal): from 60 pixels up to half the window. The current thumbnail stays where it is. Remembered.
- **Keys:** Shift + arrows (and Home, End, PgUp, PgDn) select photos in a row, like Shift+click; Ctrl + arrows move the map; «+» and «−» zoom the map wherever the focus is.
- **Open in Google Maps** in the photo menu (right click): the browser opens at the photo's point — Street View and satellite from there.
- **Camera model** in the photo view (Space), under the name: "iPhone 14 Pro Max", "Canon EOS 5D Mark II".

**Changed**
- **Markers as in Google Maps:** a red pin with a darker outline, 28 pixels instead of 40; the selected photos are blue, the current one blue and a little larger. Groups are all one size, the number exactly in the middle. Old default colors in the settings file are replaced by themselves; your own stay.
- **Blue means selected everywhere:** a blue frame around the selected thumbnails (the current one thicker), the selection rectangle on the map, the progress bar.
- **Thumbnail badges** (coordinates, address, altitude, live photo, track) are one quiet gray and differ by shape. The "not saved" pencil is white, in the bottom left corner of the picture. On the smallest strip the badges shrink a little so that all of them fit.
- The window that finishes addresses and altitudes after exit (and the batch window) is narrower and exactly as tall as its content.

**Fixed**
- Starting a drag of a photo with coordinates moved the map to its marker — there was nowhere to drag it. The map stays now.
- Ctrl+click on a thumbnail worked every other time: a slight move of the mouse turned the click into a drag that reset the selection.
- After a quick drag from the strip the thumbnails sometimes stayed dimmed.
- The start screen flashed for a moment at start although the last folder was opening.
