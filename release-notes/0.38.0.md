GeoPhotos 0.38.0 — easter eggs hidden on the map.

**Install:** download `GeoPhotos-Setup-0.38.0.exe` below and run it: it installs for you only, without administrator rights. Or take `GeoPhotos-0.38.0-win-x64.zip` and unpack it. The program is not signed: if Windows shows *"Windows protected your PC"*, click *More info → Run anyway*. If ExifTool is missing, click **Install for GeoPhotos** at the first start. **Update:** GeoPhotos 0.30.0 or later installed with the installer updates itself — the status bar says *"Version 0.38.0 is ready — restart"*. The zip version tells about it and opens this page; unpack the new zip over the old folder, settings and ExifTool stay. Details — [Installation](https://github.com/rejgan318/geophotos#installation) in the README.

**New since 0.37.0**
- **Easter eggs:** 14 photos of the Ukrainian meme "the ATB bag" are hidden on the map. Look at the right place closely enough (zoom 16 or closer) for a second — a card flies out of it with confetti: *"Easter egg 3 of 14 · place"*. The last one has its own finale. Found eggs stay on the map at close zoom as round thumbnails with a bag; a click shows the card again.
- After the first find, *Help → About* says *"Easter eggs found: N of 14"*; a click opens the list of the found places (the date of each in its hint), a place takes you there.
- Given up? The README has a spoiler section.

Windows 10/11, 64-bit. Interface: English, Russian, Ukrainian.
